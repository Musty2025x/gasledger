<h2>2. Information We Collect</h2>

<p>
Depending on how you use GasLedger, we may collect
information such as your name, email address, phone
number, business information, login credentials,
sales records, meter readings, gas quantities,
prices, inventory records, cash transactions,
POS transactions, expenses, and business reports.
</p>

<h2>3. How We Use Your Information</h2>

<p>
We use collected information to provide and maintain
GasLedger, manage user accounts, record LPG sales,
track inventory, reconcile transactions, generate
business reports, troubleshoot technical problems,
maintain security, and provide customer support.
</p>

<h2>4. How We Share Information</h2>

<p>
We do not sell your personal information. Information
may be shared with service providers where necessary
to operate, maintain, secure, or support GasLedger,
or where required by applicable law.
</p>

<h2>5. Data Security</h2>

<p>
We take reasonable technical and organizational measures
to protect information against unauthorized access,
alteration, disclosure, loss, or destruction.
</p>

<h2>6. Data Retention</h2>

<p>
We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary
to provide GasLedger, maintain business records, comply
with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and maintain
security.
