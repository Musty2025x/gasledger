1. About GasLedger

GasLedger is a business management application designed for LPG gas plant owners, managers, and attendants. The application helps businesses manage sales, gas inventory, cash and POS transactions, daily reconciliation, and business reports.

2. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use GasLedger, we may collect information such as your name, email address, phone number, business information, login credentials, sales records, meter readings, gas quantities, prices, inventory records, cash transactions, POS transactions, expenses, and business reports.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use collected information to provide and maintain GasLedger, manage user accounts, record LPG sales, track inventory, reconcile transactions, generate business reports, troubleshoot technical problems, maintain security, and provide customer support.

4. How We Share Information

We do not sell your personal information. Information may be shared with service providers where necessary to operate, maintain, secure, or support GasLedger, or where required by applicable law.

5. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, loss, or destruction.

6. Data Retention

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide GasLedger, maintain business records, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and maintain security.
